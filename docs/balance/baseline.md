## Measured anomalies (engine-side, for Gate A triage — not fixed here)

1. **Debt-interest snowball (convenience retail-steady + advisor, 50/50 runs):** cash collapses to ≈ −1.75e16 by day 365 (≈ −2e5 by day 90, then compounding). Suggests capitalized/compounding interest on unrecoverable negative cash is numerically unbounded.
2. **Grocery waste explosion (29/50 retail-steady runs):** cumulative `wasteUnits` reach ≈ 5.1e13 (median of exploded runs) up to 8.8e15 units — a stock/waste-path numeric blowup, not a measurement artifact (values are summed straight from `DailyProductReport.wasteUnits`).
3. **Convenience advisor production stall (50/50 runs):** total industrial output is zero from **day 18 through day 365 in every run** after the advisor's applied producer build (buffer-full/missing-sink shape; no repeated advisor no-op recommendations — the planner keeps proposing, output stays zero).
4. **Inverted idle control:** idle currently outlasts every managed bot on cash (first negative-cash day 60–120 vs 7–30; higher cash at every checkpoint). Managed operation destroys cash faster than doing nothing at this balance.

## Measurement limitations

- Producer-stall detection aggregates `productionReport.produced` per city per day, counted from the first applied producer build; a partial stall beside a still-producing building is invisible at this level.
- Store levels at checkpoints come from the opening ledger; harness bots never upgrade stores, so levels are always 1 at opening and thereafter.
- Runs are single-process sequential; wall-clock ≈ 3 min for the default sweep (no timing data is written to artifacts).
