# HPA-721 Starter Water Trial Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans for this bounded experiment. Steps use checkboxes. Do not commit the temporary edit.

**Goal:** Test whether one starter bottled-water chain can repay all its costs by day 365 at a retail import price of 1, without adding stores or changing how the bot plays.

**Architecture:** Reconstruct the restored economy in two isolated exports, with the identical reviewed Tier 5 operating-month diff in both. Gate the two-line recipe treatment on native scenario replays, then compare it with importing in two identical, fully staffed retail fixtures. The native industrial commands are scripted for this controlled comparison; no claim about bot adoption follows.

**Tech Stack:** Bun 1.3.14, TypeScript, Vitest, Python 3, Git.

**Spec:** The approved starter-chain design is restated under Global Constraints and in the command schedule. The unchanged gates and their limits are in `docs/balance/economy-pass.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Treatment only: `water-bottling` keeps its 10-unit raw-water input and outputs **28** bottled-water units, not 10; its recipe fee is **3**, not 5. Build one level-1 pump, one level-1 bottler and one warehouse. Keep the raw leg at level 1 and raise only the two-cell finished leg to level 3 by paying native quotes. Full construction and track outlay: **3,520**.
- Keep bottled-water import/shelf prices at **1 / 6**. Do not change material valuation, other recipes, fixed costs, default inventory, payroll, financing terms, warehouse or rail rules, scenarios, scoring, progression, bot/manager policy, or acceptance gates. No R1/R2 price bundle or setup discount.
- Run at most **14 scenario plays per arm**. Only if both pass, attempt at most **four 365-report trajectories**: import-only and local on restored convenience seeds `20260503` and `20260515`. No native progression/idle run, third arm, retry, full suite, E2E, or acceptance matrix.
- Use a fresh ignored `docs/balance/generated/hpa-721-starter-water-trial/` for immutable recipes, attempted keys, logs, raw data and results. Freeze each recipe and the inputs before it runs. The lead writes and checks all measurement and gate code. No source edits in the live worktree and no commit, push, PR update, or adoption.

## Review Focus

- Wrong source or state: verify both source trees and exact day-1 common states before either launch.
- More production without a route to retail: verify paid rail levels, actual rail pulls and pushes, and store receipts separately.
- A change that makes a teaching scenario trivial or blocks its reference: preserve the scenario gate before any annual run.
- Borrowing, year-end stock or unpaid interest mistaken for profit: report the ledger bridge and financing-neutral cash; credit no terminal stock.
- Local receipts mistaken for sales: apply the existing conservative stock bound and retain per-store reports to audit it.
