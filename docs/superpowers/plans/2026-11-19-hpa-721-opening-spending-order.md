# HPA-721 Opening Spending Order Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (native execution) or superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Follow the checkboxes in order. Do not commit or adopt any scratch code.

**Goal:** Test whether funding operations before discretionary commitments helps two Harbor convenience stores survive two payrolls and resume investment.

**Architecture:** Both arms run the same progression bot from the same post-opening snapshot with Harbor snack demand at 1.2. One scratch-only precommit check and credit restriction apply to the treatment through day 30. The native player transitions and day loop handle all actual purchases, payroll, restocks, servicing, and claims.

**Tech Stack:** Bun 1.3.14, TypeScript, Vitest server project, Python 3, Git.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-hpa-721-opening-spending-order-design.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Use two disposable exports, not a nested worktree. Copy identical scratch code into both. Control always uses the existing planner policy; from **day 31** both arms use that policy and normal borrowing. Do not change the canonical bot, manager, scoring, claims, acceptance checks, game rules, credit terms, or prices in the live worktree.
- Both arms start with the two existing level-1 stores, matching staff, stock lots, and one founding loan. Set nothing on the state after loading the verified fixture. Start with its existing **450 water / 400 snacks** reorder/target pairs; let the real bot re-enable its delegation and the manager change them normally. There is no second opening grant, free receipt, or manual stock order.
- Four matched trajectories only, control/treatment for `20260503` and `20260515`; exactly reports **1–63** and terminal state at day **64**. No retry, fallback tuning, scenario replays, ordinary annual progression runs, idle matrix, or official acceptance. Failed economics are outcomes; an integrity or execution failure stops the recipe and preserves partial evidence.
- Output under ignored `docs/balance/generated/hpa-721-opening-spending-order/` (called `$out` below). The lead authors and checks the recorder, fixture assertions and analysis. Record commands and receipts in the execution ledger, not in the canonical acceptance report.

## Review Focus

- An illegal or unquotable purchase must not consume cash, RNG, a claim, or a ledger opening; a committed purchase must apply only once (Tasks 2–3).
- A quote can be wrong at the next restock boundary or double-count loan arrears; pin tests for expiry, overlapping obligations, and the payoff of just one of two loans (Task 2).
- A second purchase can spend the first purchase's reserve; a city or store loan or the working-capital top-up can silently refill cash (Task 3).
- A fixed-start adapter can change control or accidentally keep the guard active after first payroll; check the control command pass and the first subsequent planner turn, day 36 (Task 3).
- A cash difference can be a loan or a postponed purchase; reconcile financing embedded in daily reports, separate capital outlays, and test the verdict against those traps (Task 4).
